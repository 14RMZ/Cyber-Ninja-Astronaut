Privacy Policy

Cyber Ninja Astronaut — Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyber Ninja Astronaut (the "Game"), created by RMZ, handles any information related to you when you play the game.

1. Why We Ask For Your Name & How It Is Stored

Before entering the game, you are invited to enter a Ninja Name. Here is why we ask and how it is protected:

  • Why we ask for your name: Your Ninja Name is used strictly to personalize your gaming experience — such as showing a "Welcome Back, [Name]!" badge on the menu, greeting you during the briefing, and displaying personalized high-score notes when a run ends.
  • How your name is stored: Your name is saved 100% locally on your own device inside your web browser's localStorage.
  • Zero Server Uploads: Your name is never uploaded to any remote server, external database, or third party. Nobody else can see your name unless they play on your exact device.
  • How to clear or change it: You can clear or change your stored name at any time simply by clearing your browser's history/data or clearing your browser's site storage.

2. Data We Store Locally

This game runs in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or share personal data on external servers. The only items saved in your browser's localStorage are:

  • Ninja Name — saved locally to personalize in-game greetings and death notes.
  • High Score — saved locally to remember your best score between gaming sessions.
  • Sound Volume Setting — saved locally to remember your preferred audio volume.

All of this data stays private on your own device at all times.

3. Third-Party Services

The game loads the following standard web resource:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — to display Orbitron and Share Tech Mono cyber fonts cleanly. Google handles font requests under their standard privacy terms.

No invasive advertising tracking code, analytics spyware, or data brokers are embedded in the game.

4. Cookies

This game does not use cookies. Data is stored strictly in your browser's localStorage.

5. Children's Privacy

This game is 100% family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages. No private data is ever requested or collected.
